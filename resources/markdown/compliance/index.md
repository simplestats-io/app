# Compliance

Last updated: November 21, 2026

SimpleStats is analytics that your legal team can approve without a long review. It is built and hosted in the EU, runs entirely on your server side, sets no cookies of its own and never stores IP addresses. That is why You can use it under the GDPR, the CCPA and the UK PECR **without a cookie banner for analytics**.

This page gives You and Your legal team an overview. The pages below go into detail for each region.

## Why SimpleStats Is Low-Risk to Approve

- **No cookies, no consent banner.** SimpleStats sets no cookies and stores nothing on Your visitors' devices. Tracking happens on Your server, no script runs in the visitor's browser.
- **No IP addresses stored.** IP address and User-Agent are only used for a moment to build a daily visitor hash, to detect bots and to derive the country and device type. They are never stored.
- **No cross-site tracking.** The visitor hash is built with Your application's own secret key and rotates every day, so it is different for every site. Visitors cannot be followed across days, sites or devices.
- **No selling, no sharing, no advertising.** Your data is only used to show You Your analytics. It is never sold, shared for advertising or used to train AI models.
- **EU company, EU hosting.** SimpleStats is run by Laracraft GbR in Germany and hosted by Hetzner in Germany.
- **Open source.** The code is public under the AGPL, so Your team can verify exactly what is collected. You can also [self-host](/docs/self-hosted.html) it, then no data reaches Us at all.

## Security Questionnaires and Certifications

SimpleStats itself is not SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certified. Our hosting provider Hetzner operates data centers that are certified according to ISO/IEC 27001, see [Security](/security) for details.

If Your procurement process needs a security questionnaire or further documents, email simplestats@laracraft.tech. We are happy to help.
