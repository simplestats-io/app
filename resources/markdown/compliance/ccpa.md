# CCPA Compliance

Last updated: November 21, 2026

[Compliance overview](/compliance) / CCPA

The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), gives California residents the right to know which personal information a business collects about them, to have it deleted and to opt out of its sale or sharing. "Sharing" means disclosing personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, which is exactly what most ad-driven analytics tools do.

## No Selling, No Sharing

SimpleStats does not sell personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. The data You send is only used to provide the analytics service to You. That means:

- No "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link is needed because of SimpleStats.
- Your analytics data is never combined with data from other customers or other sources.
- Your data is never used for advertising and never used to train AI models.

## SimpleStats as Your Service Provider

Under the CCPA, We act as Your service provider. We process the personal information You send only on Your behalf and only to provide the service, as set out in Our [Data Processing Agreement](/dpa). We do not retain, use or disclose it for any other purpose.

## Little Personal Information to Begin With

- **IP address and User-Agent** are processed only for a moment and are never stored.
- **Visitor hash:** built in Your application with Your own secret key, it rotates every day and cannot be linked to a person.
- **No cookies** and no other identifiers on the visitor's device.
- **No tracking across sites or devices.**

The full list of data categories is part of the [Data Processing Agreement](/dpa), Section 3.

## Requests from Consumers

California residents send their requests to You as the business. If a request concerns a registered user of Your application, contact Us with the user identifier and We assist You. When You delete a project, a team or Your account, the associated analytics data is deleted.

## Other US State Privacy Laws

Several other US states have passed privacy laws that follow a similar model, for example Virginia, Colorado and Connecticut. Because SimpleStats does not sell data, does not use it for targeted advertising and does not build profiles, the same principles apply there.

This page explains how SimpleStats is built. It is not legal advice. Whether the CCPA applies to Your business depends, among other things, on Your revenue and the number of California residents whose data You process.
