These guidelines explain how You may use them, so that people always know whether they are dealing with Us or with someone else.

## What You May Do

- Say that You use SimpleStats, that Your analytics are "powered by SimpleStats", or that Your product integrates with SimpleStats.
- Write about SimpleStats in blog posts, tutorials, videos and talks, and use the name to refer to Us and Our software.
- Self-host the unmodified SimpleStats for Your own projects and keep the original name and logo in that installation.
- Use the name in a subdomain of Your own installation, for example simplestats.yourcompany.com.
- Name a package or integration in a way that makes the relationship clear without suggesting it is Ours, for example "Statamic integration for SimpleStats".

## What You May Not Do

- Use SimpleStats, the logo or a confusingly similar name (variations, misspellings, translations) as the name, logo or domain of Your own product, fork, company or hosted service.
- Offer a hosted or managed analytics service to third parties under the SimpleStats name or logo.
- Distribute or publicly host a modified version of SimpleStats under the SimpleStats name or logo without Our prior written permission. Without it, replace them with Your own. With it, use Your own logo and mention SimpleStats only to describe where the software comes from.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests Your product, service or content is official, certified or endorsed by Us when it is not.
- Display the SimpleStats name or logo more prominently than Your own product or company name.
- Register domains, social media accounts or other trademarks containing SimpleStats or a confusingly similar name.

## Questions

If You are unsure whether Your use is fine, or You want permission for a use not covered here, email simplestats@laracraft.tech. We are happy to help.
